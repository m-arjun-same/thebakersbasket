Some answers

How do I order?

DM us on Instagram @thebakersbasket, call or WhatsApp +92 321 5375875, or email abeera.wajid71@gmail.com. Tell us the cake, the date and the occasion — we confirm the price on enquiry, nothing is priced blind.

Where are you based?

Lahore, Pakistan. We are a home kitchen run by a mother and dentist duo.

Do you deliver?

Yes — pickup in Lahore, or delivery via Uber car. Please note: no COD (cash on delivery) is available.

What do you make?

Custom celebration cakes, customised bento cakes, cupcakes (birthday, graduation, Eid and seasonal boxes), cupcake baskets and gift boxes with cards.

Why are there no prices on the site?

Every cake is customised, so the price depends on size, design and date. We confirm your exact price when you enquire — we never invent a price here.
